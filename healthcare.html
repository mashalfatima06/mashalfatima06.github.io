MashaAI Connect is an AI receptionist and appointment setter for clinics that answers every patient call, books the appointment directly into your scheduling system, and sends automated reminders before each visit — reducing missed calls and no-shows without adding front desk staff.

Why missed calls cost clinics more than they realize

A ringing phone that goes to voicemail during a busy clinic day is a patient who, in many cases, simply calls the next practice on the list. Between patient intake calls, rescheduling requests, and reminder calls, front desk staff are often stretched thin — and every unanswered call is a booking that doesn't happen.

How MashaAI Connect works for a clinic

1. AI answers every inbound call

Whether it's during business hours, after hours, or during a lunch rush, the AI answers immediately — no hold music, no voicemail.

2. Patient booking and rescheduling

The AI checks real-time calendar availability, books new patient appointments, and handles rescheduling requests in the same call.

3. Automated reminders reduce no-shows

Before each appointment, the AI sends a reminder call or message, and can rebook on the spot if the patient needs to reschedule — directly addressing the biggest driver of no-shows.

4. Routing for urgent or complex calls

Calls that need a human — clinical questions, urgent concerns, or complex billing issues — are routed to your front desk team with full context.

Built for the way clinics actually operate

  • Works alongside your front desk team — it handles routine call volume, they handle patients in the office
  • Fits dental practices, medical clinics, aesthetic clinics, and specialist offices
  • Covers after-hours and weekend calls, when many patients try to reach a practice
  • Reduces the administrative load that leads to staff burnout at busy practices
