Privacy Policy

How we handle your data.

Last updated: September 19, 2026

MashaAI Connect (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the website and AI voice agent services described on this site. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide. When you request a demo, contact us, or use our services, we may collect your name, email address, company name, industry, phone number, and your message.

Call and interaction data. When our AI agents handle calls on your behalf, we may process call audio and transcriptions, lead contact details (such as name, phone, and email), and records of the conversation outcome. This data is processed on behalf of the business using our service.

Technical data. We may automatically collect analytics such as pages visited, browser type, device information, and IP address to operate and improve our website.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to provide and maintain our services, respond to inquiries and demo requests, operate our AI calling workflows, improve our products, monitor and secure our systems, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

3. How We Share Information

We share information only as needed to provide our services: with service providers who process data on our behalf (such as hosting, telephony, and email providers), with the businesses we serve when processing their leads and conversations, when required by law, or to protect our legal rights.

4. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide our services, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, and comply with applicable laws. Call recordings and transcriptions are retained in accordance with the preferences of the business using our service.

5. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the information we hold against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission over the internet is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port the personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website may link to third-party services such as LinkedIn and Instagram. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices. We encourage you to review their policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any updated version on this page and update the “Last updated” date above. Please review this page periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at info@mashaaiconnect.com.
