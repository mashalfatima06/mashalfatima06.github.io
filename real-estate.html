Why speed to lead matters more in real estate than almost any other industry

Real estate leads are uniquely perishable. A buyer who fills out a form on a Zillow listing or a Facebook ad is often looking at three or four other properties or agents at the same time. Response time research across industries consistently shows contact and conversion rates fall sharply once follow-up passes the 5-minute mark, and keep falling the longer a lead waits. For a 5-20 agent team relying on paid lead sources, that gap is where deals are lost - not to a better agent, but to whoever called first.

How MashaAI Connect works for a real estate team

1. Lead comes in from your paid source

Whether the lead comes from Zillow Premier Agent, a Facebook or Google ad, or your own website form, MashaAI Connect receives it instantly.

2. AI calls within 60 seconds

The AI places a natural-sounding call to the lead, introduces itself, and starts a qualifying conversation - no hold music, no delay.

3. Buyer or seller qualification

The AI asks the questions your team actually needs answered: budget range, financing or pre-approval status, timeline to buy or sell, and property preferences. It handles common objections the way a trained ISA would.

4. Viewing or call booked automatically

Once qualified, the AI checks your team's live calendar and books a property viewing or a call with the assigned agent, then sends confirmation and reminder messages to cut down on no-shows.
