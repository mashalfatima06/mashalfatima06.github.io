Why It Matters

Speed to Lead Decides Who Gets the Deal

Real estate leads are uniquely perishable - here's why.

The First 5 Minutes

Response time research consistently shows contact and conversion rates fall sharply once follow-up passes the 5-minute mark - and keep falling the longer a lead waits.

Everyone's Calling at Once

A buyer who fills out a form on a listing or ad is usually looking at several other properties or agents at the same time. First contact often wins the relationship.

After-Hours Leads Are Real Leads

Most real estate inquiries come in evenings and weekends - exactly when a 5-20 agent team is least likely to have someone free to answer the phone.
