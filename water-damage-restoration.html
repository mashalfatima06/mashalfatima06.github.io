MashaAI Connect is an AI emergency call answering and dispatch system for water damage restoration companies that answers every inbound call, captures the loss location and property details, and gets a crew dispatched - so the job goes to whoever answers first, not whoever calls back.

Why speed decides who wins the job in restoration

Water damage is one of the most time-sensitive calls in home services. A homeowner or property manager dealing with an active flood or burst pipe is rarely calling just one company - they're calling several, and the job typically goes to whoever picks up and shows urgency first. A missed call at 2am or during an active jobsite isn't just a lost lead, it's a job that's already gone to a competitor by the time you call back.

How MashaAI Connect works for a restoration company

1. AI answers every inbound call, day or night

Whether it's the middle of the night, a weekend, or while your crew is already on a job, the AI answers immediately - no hold music, no voicemail, no lost lead.

2. Structured loss intake

The AI asks the questions your dispatcher needs - source of water, affected rooms, category of water, whether the property is occupied, and the caller's contact and address details - so your crew shows up prepared, not guessing.

3. Immediate dispatch or callback

Based on availability, the AI can confirm a crew is on the way, schedule an assessment window, or immediately route true emergencies to your on-call team with full context from the call.

4. Follow-up on quotes and assessments

For jobs that need a full assessment or insurance scope before work starts, the AI can follow up on outstanding quotes so leads don't go cold while you're focused on active jobs.

Built for the way restoration companies actually operate

  • Works alongside your dispatcher and crews - it handles the first response, they handle the job
  • Fits water damage, flood, mold remediation, and general mitigation companies
  • Covers the after-hours and weekend calls that make up a large share of true emergencies
