MashaAI Connect is a white-label AI appointment setter for marketing and sales agencies that calls each client's new leads within 60 seconds, qualifies them, and books the appointment directly into the client's calendar — configurable per client account, and resold under the agency's own brand.

The gap between running ads and closing deals

Agencies are frequently judged on lead volume and cost per lead, but clients ultimately care about closed deals. When a client's internal team responds to leads slowly, the agency's campaign gets blamed even though the ad performed exactly as designed. Adding AI-powered follow-up closes that gap, and turns it into a service the agency controls and profits from.

How MashaAI Connect works for an agency

1. Configure per client account

Each client gets their own instance — lead source connection, calendar integration, and a script tailored to their business and offer.

2. AI calls every new lead within 60 seconds

Whether leads come from Facebook ads, Google ads, or the client's website, MashaAI Connect calls, qualifies, and books automatically.

3. Reporting the agency can show clients

Call recordings, transcripts, and booking data give agencies concrete proof of what happens after the ad click — a stronger retention story than lead volume alone.

4. Resold under the agency's brand

The service can be presented to end clients under the agency's own name, positioned as part of a full-funnel offer rather than a third-party tool.
